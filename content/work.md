## 2003

This is the first time I encounter a computer. I am about four feet tall, and I'm in my school's _Computer Lab_. This computer is white in colour. I am in utter disbelief – I have seen these on TV. I know my dad has one at his work. This thing can do so many things!

Over the year I learn that this computer runs something called Windows 98. You do not just turn the power off on this computer, you have to go to _Start > Shutdown_. How funny.

MS Paint is awesome. MS Office makes me feel like a hacker.

## 2004

I learn about [Logo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logo_(programming_language)). When you open the program called PC LOGO, a black screen takes over the interface. On the center of this screen is a pixelated triangle.

A **? █** symbol on the bottom of the screen awaits my instructions. [This article by Susan Pal](https://susam.net/fd-100.html) is a wonderful recall of how first experiences with Logo can be.

I get so good at it. I built shapes, flowers, landscapes and faces. One landscape had a sun, a river, a hut and 3 birds. Another one had many many flowers in it.
